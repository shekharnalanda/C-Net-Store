# C-Net Store — Project Scope

## Product

C-Net Store is a multi-vendor marketplace for Bihar Sharif combining:

- Shopping and local retail
- Grocery ordering
- Restaurant and food delivery
- C-Net managed delivery and seller-managed delivery

## Channels

- Customer website: https://cnetstore.mciedu.com
- Customer app: Android and iOS
- Seller app: Android and iOS
- Delivery partner app: Android and iOS
- Super Admin web panel

## Commercial rules

- Online payments only; Cash on Delivery is disabled.
- Commission, delivery fee, minimum order and service radius are configurable.
- Sellers and restaurants require admin approval before going live.
